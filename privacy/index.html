Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

The short version: Vagary keeps your walks on your phone.

  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Your location, steps and workouts are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone.

This policy explains what information the Vagary app ("Vagary", "the app", "we") uses, why, and the choices you have. Vagary is a walking tracker made by an independent developer, Alejandro Lopez.

Information the app uses

InformationWhy Vagary uses itWhere it goes
Precise and approximate location To center the map where you are and, while a workout is running, to draw your route and work out distance and pace. Stays on your device. The route is saved with the workout.
Physical activity (step count) To count your steps during a workout, using your phone's built-in step sensor. Stays on your device.
Workouts and journey progress To show your recent walks (route, distance, time, pace and steps) and your progress on journeys like the Camino de Santiago. Stays on your device, in the app's private storage.

Vagary uses your location only while the app is open. It doesn't ask for background location and doesn't track you when the app is closed.

Vagary doesn't ask for your name, email address, contacts or photos, and it doesn't use your camera. It has no sign-in and no user accounts.

Services Vagary relies on

Map tiles: OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap

The maps in Vagary are loaded from OpenFreeMap and use map data from OpenStreetMap contributors. To show a map, your phone downloads map images for the area on screen, usually the area around you. Like any website request, this shares your device's IP address and which map area is being loaded with OpenFreeMap. Vagary doesn't send your saved workouts, routes or step counts to OpenFreeMap or anyone else.

Android location services

Vagary gets your location from your phone's location services, which on most Android phones are provided by Google Play services. Google handles that information under the Google Privacy Policy and your device's location settings.

Android backup

If you've turned on backup on your phone, Android may include Vagary's saved data in your device backup to your Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is managed by Google, not by us, and you can turn it off in your phone's Settings under Google > Backup.

What we don't do

  • We don't run servers that collect your data. We never receive your location, routes, steps or workouts.
  • We don't sell, rent or share personal information.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Permissions

  • Location: to show where you are on the map and record your route.
  • Physical activity: to count your steps.
  • Internet: to load map tiles.

You can allow or deny these at any time in your phone's Settings under Apps > Vagary > Permissions. If you deny location, Vagary can't track your route. If you deny physical activity, steps won't be counted.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your workouts and journey progress stay on your phone until you delete them. To delete a single workout, tap it in the Recent list and tap the delete icon. To delete everything, clear the app's storage in Settings under Apps > Vagary > Storage, or uninstall the app. Since we don't hold a copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Security

Vagary stores your data in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. We recommend protecting your phone with a screen lock.

Children

Vagary isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Because the app doesn't send personal information to us, we don't hold any information about children.

Changes to this policy

If Vagary changes how it handles information, we'll update this page and the effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
